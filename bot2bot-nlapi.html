#bot2bot #NLAPI

March 28, 2017

Hi Angik!

Yes, #bot2bot is an hot topic. There are here both business (monetization) and technical (API) open topics.

What is now prevalent is what I call server-centric model (with again both business/tech implications). Goshup pubsub proposal is in that way.

immo, the challenging / innovative realm is pure #p2p (w negotiation) #bot2bot paradigm and I’m especially interested in #NLAPI (Natural Language interchange bot communication)…
Incoming #bot2bot features are: automatic negotiation and introspection (a bot must report reasons why he delegated to a third party bots, and the bot to bot communication would be in pseudo-natural langauge)…
